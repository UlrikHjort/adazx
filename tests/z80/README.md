ZEXDOC / ZEXALL Z80 instruction exercisers by Frank D. Cringle (GPL v2),
CP/M .COM programs. bin/z80_test runs them with a minimal CP/M BDOS.
zexdoc checks the documented flags, zexall also the undocumented ones.

zexall.z80 / zexdoc.z80 are their sources (from
https://github.com/agn453/ZEXALL; the .COM files are byte-identical to the
ones built there), and COPYING is the GNU GPL version 2 they are under.
The Fuse test data in fuse/ (tests.in, tests.expected) comes from the Fuse
emulator, also GPL v2.  These files are separate works kept next to
AdaZX's MIT-licensed code; their licence is theirs.
